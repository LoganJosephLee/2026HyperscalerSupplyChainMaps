These six companies — Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Oracle, CoreWeave — are building the data centres that run AI. They buy almost nothing directly: chips are designed by one company, printed by another, packaged by a third, fitted with memory from a fourth, bolted into racks by a fifth, and powered, cooled and shipped by others again.

This map shows those relationships, but only where a company said so in a filing to the US financial regulator. Click any line and you get the exact sentence, the form it came from, and a link to the filing itself. If a relationship isn't here, it usually means nobody had to disclose it — not that it doesn't exist.

Companies are grouped by what they do, and each group has a plain-English explanation — click one in the legend.

Click any edge to see the sentence that put it there — verbatim, with the filing it came from and a link to that filing on EDGAR.
