Who builds the AI cloud?

Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta and Oracle are racing to build AI data centers. What goes inside them comes from a long chain of other companies: chip designers, chip factories, memory makers, server builders, power plants. This map shows who supplies what, layer by layer, and every connection links to the sentence that states it.

How every claim here is sourced

Each relationship on this map is backed by at least one quote, copied word for word, with a link to where it was published. Each quote is labelled with the kind of source it came from:

Quotes from SEC filings are matched against a saved copy of the filing every time the map is rebuilt; a quote that is not in the filing stops the build. Quotes from web pages are checked by fetching the page and searching for the exact words, and each one shows when it was last checked.

A missing connection does not mean two companies don't do business. Many suppliers are never named in public: US companies must report when one customer makes up 10% or more of their revenue, but smaller relationships usually go unnamed, and many deals are never announced.
